## 1. The Resilience Problem

A critique of pure mathematical tokenomics is that **fixed economic constants cannot survive dynamic human history**. If an economy faces:
- A sudden global pandemic,
- Prolonged warfare,
- An acute liquidity crunch, or
- Runaway supply shocks,

a rigid parameter set ($\Theta = 1,000,000\text{ OEQ}$, 40/20/40 fee split) could either starve households during a depression or overheat markets during a speculative boom.

The **Qmoosa Omni-Equality Protocol** addresses this through the **Dynamic Adaptability Engine** (`core/tokenomics/dynamic_governance.py`).

Parameter transitions are **NEVER dictated by a centralized central bank, algorithmic dictator, or billionaire cartel**.

1. **Trigger Proposal**: Any verified Soulbound citizen or decentralized oracle group can propose an emergency regime transition with verifiable economic data.
2. **Soulbound Democratic Balloting**: Every verified human citizen holds exactly **1.0 voting power** via NIST FIPS 204 ML-DSA-65 credentials. Wealth balances provide zero additional vote weight.
3. **Supermajority Consensus**: If $\ge 60\%$ of voting citizens ratify the emergency shift, the smart contract parameters adapt dynamically, adjusting $\Theta$ and split ratios across the next block epoch.
4. **Sunset Timelock**: All crisis regimes carry a mandatory 90-day review period, after which the system automatically evaluates equilibrium restoration.
